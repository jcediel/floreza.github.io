About Floreza

Fresh cut flowers, sourced globally

We import the finest and freshest cut flowers from all around the world and distribute them all across the United States, with a focus on quality, variety and service.

Our team works directly with you to design a cost-efficient floral program for your grocery or supermarket stores.
